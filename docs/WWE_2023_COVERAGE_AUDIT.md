## Programmes, regionals and promotions outside the main-parent count

- **2023 Panini Instant / Instant Weekly Pass:** the 2023 index explicitly lists this programme. It is not present as a 2023 annual parent in the current catalogue. Enumerate its individual physical issues, distinguish any 2022–2023 continuing run, and resolve overlaps before importing.
- **Regional Adrenalyn XL:** 2024 is already an open coverage area. This audit did not confirm a separately titled 2023 edition; do not manufacture an annual release from a later year's product.
- **Promotions and multisport WWE subjects:** Father's Day, National/event issues and other promotional candidates need their own identity/manufacturer audit. Their possible existence does not increase the seven confirmed main-parent count. No worldwide/year-completeness claim is made.
- FOTL, Blaster, Under Card, Asia/Tmall, Lucky Envelopes and Premium Factory Set should be reviewed as editions of verified parent products unless source-backed design identities justify a different relationship. Digital-only blockchain releases and unrelated AEW/Leaf/independent wrestling products are excluded from this WWE parent queue.
