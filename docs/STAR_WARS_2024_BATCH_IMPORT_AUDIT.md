## Identity and source limits

**Chrome Black:** 100 base, 95 inserts and 229 autograph designs. The manufacturer prints ALS-KK twice on page 5 for Keira Knightley / Sabé and Katy Kartwheel / Rio Durant. Both printed numbers remain ALS-KK, with distinct internal signer suffixes. They are not a dual autograph. The 22 dual and 18 triple signer rows combine into 11 and six designs. The ALS-KF character is printed simply F; no name correction inferred. Packaging / Sell Sheet headings contain no checklist rows. All 60 odds rows become rules; Galactic Factions alone explicitly states numbered to 99, retained as a subset cap. No other Black serial caps inferred.

**High-Tek:** Seven separate 100-card base patterns produce 700 explicit identities; 25 black-and-white variations, 75 inserts and 82 autograph designs complete 882 designs. The ten dual and three double-sided designs each have one printed label followed by two signer/character pairs, giving 895 subject/signer rows from 882 printed labels. Each pair is one card. All seven #1 cards have separate ownership IDs. Fantastek prints S-M and S-ZB rather than F-prefixes; those printed numbers remain unchanged. The odds file is titled 2024 NYCC Star Wars (Hi-Tek ENT), but Topps links it under regular 2024 High-Tek and its distributor SKU 5874 matches the Hobby product. This is not used to invent a separate NYCC release. Its generic BASE CARDS PATTERN 1:12 label cannot be assigned to one of seven patterns and remains source-only, along with four sketch rates. Other 63 odds rows become rules. The manufacturer guide explicitly confirms 31 base-pattern caps: four patterns have /50,/25,/10,/5,/1; Rebel has /10,/5,/1; each Death Star pattern has /25,/10,/5,/1. The GOLD FOIL FOIL label is retained while its cap reconciles to the guide's Gold Rainbow Foil. Guide pack counts differ from the product's box configuration; only explicit cap ladders are used, with PDF pack rates retained verbatim. No autograph caps or subject-specific grids inferred. The official 2024 title and 8 January 2025 date take precedence over inconsistent generic Product Year metadata.

**Galaxy:** 100 base, 95 inserts, 70 autograph designs. Twelve dual, twelve triple and sixteen quad signer rows combine into six, four and four designs. Source artwork credits remain searchable in the roster field. Base #93 has separate Falcon and Rising PDF text cells; they join as the visually confirmed title Falcon Rising, with Mark Chilcott as artist. SITH AS-1 and ARMOR AS-1 remain different identities despite shared printed numbers. The 123 sketch and six animator names remain rosters. All 95 odds rows are retained; five sketch/Blank Canvas rates are source-only. Other 90 odds rows become rules, with two additional explicit guide-only /5 rules for the triple (printed Refractror) and quad Refractor editions; no unnamed pack-rate mapping is inferred for those rules. The guide confirms 33 subset caps in total. Labels including Annversary, Bue Refractor, X-Fractor Refractor and Art of the Naboo remain source evidence without guessed corrections.

All **1,571 added variant rows are checklist identity placeholders** with null caps. The 65 explicitly verified serial-cap facts live in subset rules, with no expansion to subject-by-parallel variant grids. Unnumbered sketch subjects remain unenumerated; no licensed card images added.
